Motorcycle Restoration (1992 CB400SF)

Restored a 1992 CB400SF (NC31) Motorcycle

What this demonstrates

  • Self-directed learning: restored a 1992 CB400SF from zero prior experience by researching, planning, and executing repairs end-to-end.
  • Electrical upgrades: installed LED headlights and a wireless charger with clean wiring and functional testing.
  • Structured troubleshooting: resolved non-start and drivability issues via systematic checks (pilot jet tuning, spark plugs, air filter, fuel delivery).
  • Safety-critical maintenance: rebuilt key wear items (clutch plates, fork seals, chain/sprockets, brake pads) with careful inspection and validation.
  • Problem Solving and Innovation: Overcame unexpected issues due to age. E.g. Corrosion bonded parts, inaccessible proprietary tools, etc.

Summary

Motorcycle was non-starting and sitting for 7 years. The clutch was disassembled, forks leaking oil, tyres worn and hard, chains and sprockets worn, hand grips and foot peg grips hard and slippery, coolant loop corroded and leaking, spark plugs worn, throttle and clutch wires rough and sticking and lighting and horn wires corroded.

I repaired and restored each of these issues myself from zero experience. I also wired a LED headlight and wireless charger using a multimeter and wiring diagram.
