Remote-Controlled Fire Truck Prototype

Designed and constructed a Remote Controlled Fire Truck

What this demonstrates

  • End-to-end mechatronics integration: electronics + mechanical build + software control working as one system.
  • Circuitry fundamentals applied in practice: power delivery, grounding, signal integrity basics, safe wiring/routing.
  • Hardware debugging discipline: isolate variables, test hypotheses, iterate, and validate fixes with repeatable checks.
  • Reliability improvements: addressing intermittent faults, loose connections, power brownouts, mechanical binding, etc.
  • Quick and Self sufficient learner: researching, studying and implementing new concepts to fulfill the given task.

Summary

In this assignment, we were tasked to design and construct a remote controlled fire truck satisfying size, weight, functionality, cost and resource constraints. Then to write a report detailing the entire design and construction process.

The task was split into 4 subsystems: Structural, Fluid Delivery, Control, and Drive. I was responsible for the Control subsystem. That is, I was responsible for all the circuitry, controls, code and final construction.
